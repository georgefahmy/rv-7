## Part 2: Required FAA & State Documentation Checklist
Before anyone flies, you must have these exact physical and digital files organized.

| Document Name | Purpose | Where to File / Keep |
|---|---|---|
| Articles of Organization | Creates the LLC as a legal business entity. | Your State's Secretary of State |
| EIN Assignment Letter | Federal Tax ID number used to open the LLC bank account. | IRS (Online, instant) |
| LLC Operating Agreement | The internal rules governing member buy-ins, scheduling, and billing. | Keep on file internally |
| FAA Form 8050-2 (Bill of Sale) | Transfers the RV-7 from Your Individual Name to The LLC Name. | Mail to FAA Aircraft Registry |
| FAA Form 8050-1 (Registration) | Registers the N-number under the new LLC. | Mail to FAA Aircraft Registry |
| FAA Form 8050-24 (LLC Statement) | Certifies U.S. citizenship of the LLC members (Mandatory for LLCs). | Mail to FAA Aircraft Registry with above |
| Aircraft Operating Limitations | The original paper documents issued by your DAR when the plane was certified. | Must remain in the aircraft |
| Insurance Policy Amendment | Rewritten policy naming the LLC as owner and listing all co-owners as approved pilots. | Keep on file / in aircraft |
